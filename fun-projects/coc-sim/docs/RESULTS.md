# TH8 war study: results

Generated by `npm run sim -- study` on 2026-10-27 (20 min, 3 arms-race rounds).
Data: clash-of-clans-data (npm, MIT) — community-maintained game data v0.19.0. Everything at TH8 max (Lab 6, Hero Hall 2 → one hero slot, Blacksmith 1).

**Read this first.** These numbers come from a simulator whose mechanics are partly estimated (see the confidence table at the end).
Rankings (which attack beats which base) are more trustworthy than exact percentages. Calibrate with real replays (docs/CALIBRATION.md) to tighten them.

Each attack scouts every base and attacks from its best of 8 directions; results use 4 runs per direction with human-like deploy imprecision.

## What this run says (and doesn't)

- **Mass Dragons is the best TH8 war attack in the sim.** It 3-stars every base tested,
  including bases evolved specifically against it. That matches the 2025–26 guides calling
  dragons the most reliable TH8 3-star army. DragLoon and GoWiPe come next.
- **Bases can beat hogs, not dragons.** Three rounds of evolution cut Mass Hogs from 100%
  to 0% 3-stars and GoVaHo to 50% on the round-3 base, but no layout stopped a tuned dragon
  attack. Pick your war base for the attack your opponents actually use. Against TH8
  clans that's mostly dragons, so test candidates with `mass-dragon` first.
- **The Queen wins the single hero slot** for every tuned attack. Frozen Arrow appears in 6 of 8
  setups, and Magic Mirror is the strongest second piece.
- **The absolute numbers are too generous to the attacker.** Players describe GoWiPe as hard
  at TH8, but here it 3-stars everything. Uncalibrated mechanics (pathing, retarget
  pauses, hitboxes) and a scripted attacker that never panics make attacks too efficient.
  Trust the order, not the percentages, until real replays calibrate it (docs/CALIBRATION.md).
